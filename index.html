Working Papers

Working Paper

Declining Business Dynamism in Korea: Shocks, Responsiveness, and the Role of Mature Firms

with Yoonsoo Lee and Jungmin Han

The paper studies the sharp decline in Korean business dynamism and asks whether it reflects smaller idiosyncratic productivity shocks or weaker responsiveness of employment and exit to productivity. The analysis emphasizes employment adjustment, survival, firm age, job reallocation, and aggregate productivity implications.

Working Paper · Preliminary and incomplete draft (October 29, 2026)

What Happens When Mortgage Credit Stops at a Price Threshold? Housing Transactions, Reallocation, and Welfare in Korea

Taehoon Kim

Abstract. This paper studies how price-threshold mortgage restrictions affect housing market activity, housing allocation, and welfare. Focusing on Korea’s December 2019 ban on purchase mortgages for apartments above KRW 1.5 billion, I document sharp bunching below the threshold and a significant decline in sales of exposed apartments, while relative prices respond more moderately. Motivated by these patterns, I develop a heterogeneous-household model with indivisible housing and borrowing constraints.
