Working Papers

Working Paper

Declining Business Dynamism in Korea: Shocks, Responsiveness, and the Role of Mature Firms

with Yoonsoo Lee and Jungmin Han

The paper studies the sharp decline in Korean business dynamism and asks whether it reflects smaller idiosyncratic productivity shocks or weaker responsiveness of employment and exit to productivity. The analysis emphasizes employment adjustment, survival, firm age, job reallocation, and aggregate productivity implications.

Work in Progress · Preliminary title

Mortgage Constraints, Housing Adjustment, and Household Welfare

This project studies nonlinear mortgage constraints, bunching around policy thresholds, discrete housing adjustment, equilibrium house prices and allocations, aggregate consumption/assets, welfare, and policy counterfactuals.
